Hachibeto is a music artist whose identity is woven into the geometry of eight — the endless curve, the twin circles, the infinity loop. Every track is a journey from one lobe to the other and back, a sound that turns in on itself and resurfaces changed.

Drawing on the visual tension of curves that cross themselves — the figure 8, the ∞, the X — Hachibeto builds sonic worlds that feel simultaneously grounded and unresolved, always mid-loop.
